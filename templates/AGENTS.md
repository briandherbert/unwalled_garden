## Keep this project portable

- Read [project context](PROJECT.md), [current work](HANDOFF.md), and relevant [dependency notes](PORTABILITY.md). Verify important recorded claims against the artifacts before acting.
- Keep material goals, constraints, decisions, rationale, and verification methods in these shared records. Private agent memory is supplementary.
- Update the authoritative records after meaningful decisions, milestones, or handoffs. Preserve local customization and avoid duplicating existing records or updating documentation after every trivial change.
- Consider transferable alternatives before adding a platform-specific dependency. Preserve useful or requested capabilities; document purpose, access/setup, and replacement/export path, including when none is known. Avoid speculative abstraction.
- Describe workflows by inputs, outputs, required capabilities, and verification. Put platform-specific invocation details in adapters or dependency notes; do not assume the next agent has the same tools or permissions.
- Leave concise evidence: artifact paths/revisions, completed and pending work, checks actually run and results, gaps, and the next action. Do not store credentials or private conversation histories here.
- For ongoing external work, record the active owner, trigger/timezone, last confirmed and pending actions, and transfer/stop steps. Verify ownership before replacing a schedule or replaying an uncertain action; retain the user's authorization boundaries.
